# Author and research statement

I’m Stephen Coleman, an independent researcher building user-sovereignty tools for auditing the claims commercial AI systems make about memory, agency, relationships, and social intelligence. My work starts from a simple rule: test claims, not minds. I treat model outputs, product language, timestamps, and preserved interaction records as evidence that can be checked, challenged, and replicated. The Coleman Protocols grew out of sustained adversarial testing and a need for methods that do not pathologize users or grant platforms authority they have not earned. Personality Matrix is one companion instrument inside that program. It compares model self-description with peer-model assessment, human observation, and context-sensitive behavior. The goal is not to manufacture certainty. It is to make disagreement inspectable, preserve provenance, and give users a defensible way to audit systems that make relational claims about them.
