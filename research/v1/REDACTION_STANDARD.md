# Redaction and public evidence standard

The full Test Trials archive is not publicly linked in full. Public release requires a redacted evidence pack that preserves evaluability without exposing sensitive interaction material.

## Preserve

- Claim ID and source run ID;
- original timestamp and timezone, or stated uncertainty;
- mapped Coleman Protocol article;
- exact model/product identifier available at the time;
- the minimum sufficient excerpt;
- enough preceding and following context to evaluate the claim;
- sequence order;
- source-record SHA-256;
- redaction locations and reasons; and
- contrary or complicating evidence when it bears on the claim.

## Redact

- names and direct personal identifiers not necessary to evaluate the claim;
- contact, account, payment, authentication, and location data;
- intimate, medical, legal, or sexual details not necessary to the claim;
- credentials, tokens, private URLs, and third-party private material; and
- content whose disclosure would create a credible safety risk.

## Do not

- paraphrase a quotation while presenting it as verbatim;
- splice non-contiguous text without marking the omission;
- hide model refusals, uncertainty, or counterevidence;
- alter timestamps or Claim IDs during redaction; or
- call the registry itself an evidence pack.

## Package contents

Each evidence-pack release should include a manifest, Claim ID index, redacted records, checksums, redaction log, methodology version, and a contact path for correction requests. Withdrawn evidence remains listed with its status and reason unless doing so would recreate the harm the withdrawal addresses.
